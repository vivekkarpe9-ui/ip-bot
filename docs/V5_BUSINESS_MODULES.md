# iP Bot V5 Business Modules

All modules reuse the same platform primitives: Room, Agent, Team, Task, Workflow, Research, Customer, Product/Service, Project, Finance, Approval and Reporting.

## Solar
Lead management, customer consultation, system sizing inputs, quotation workflow, subsidy information research, project tracking, installation/service follow-up and business reporting.

## Travel
Trip research, itinerary planning, customer groups, hotel/vendor comparison, transport planning, quotation, booking workflow, trip updates and trip P&L.

## Shopping/Fashion
Catalog, supplier research, product listing, pricing/margin analysis, orders, fulfillment coordination, returns and marketing analytics.

## Call Centre
Enquiries, ticketing, routing, follow-up, call summaries, quality analytics and escalation.

## HR
Recruitment workflow, candidate organization, onboarding, attendance/leave administration, training and reporting. Employment-impacting decisions remain subject to human review.

## Marketing
Campaign planning, content drafts, creative briefs, lead attribution, performance analytics and approval-based publishing.

## Operations
Projects, tasks, inventory, procurement, vendors, scheduling, delivery tracking and operational reporting.

## Custom Business
Users can create a custom room and define its objective, entities, agents, workflows, KPIs and permissions without changing the core platform.
