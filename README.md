# iP Bot

AI-powered business operating system for agent teams, business rooms, automation, research, analytics, and business management.

## Vision

iP Bot coordinates specialized AI agents across reusable business rooms such as Solar, Travel, Shopping, HR, Call Centre, Marketing, Finance, Operations, and custom businesses.

## Core architecture

- Master Agent and specialized Agent Teams
- Business Rooms with reusable workflows
- Human approval and permission center
- Deep Research and evidence-backed analysis
- Customer, sales, inventory, project, HR, and operations management
- Website/app business factory
- Gmail and spreadsheet reporting integrations
- Business dashboards, P&L, costing, break-even, and scenario analysis
- Audit logs and agent activity tracking

## Initial build principles

1. Mobile-first experience.
2. Modular rooms and agents so new businesses can be added without rewriting the platform.
3. Human approval for consequential financial, contractual, employment, publishing, and external actions.
4. Secrets and OAuth credentials are never hard-coded.
5. Research claims should be traceable to sources.
6. Keep the core platform separate from business-specific room modules.
