## User action policy
No user action is required for the local/demo implementation. Ask only when a real account connection, OAuth consent, production deployment, model/provider selection, or consequential external action is actually needed.

## Important product boundary
The platform may automate research, drafting, analysis, coordination and routine workflows. Financial transactions, contractual commitments, employment-impacting decisions, external publishing and other consequential actions remain subject to explicit permissions and configurable human approval.
